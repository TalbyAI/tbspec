# Semantic coverage audit: implementation stories 43–84

Audit date: 2026-10-07

## Scope and method

This audit maps every MVP implementation story numbered 43 through 84 to the semantic obligations in the acceptance criteria of the 20 implementation tickets. Acceptance criteria are numbered sequentially within each ticket, starting at AC1. A “User stories covered” reference is an index only; it does not establish coverage by itself. Blanket normative references remain binding, but this audit distinguishes direct ticket acceptance/evidence from behavior left to shared contracts or later tickets.

**Explicit** means the ticket criteria directly capture the operative behavior. **Delegated-only** means the right contract is referenced or the behavior broadly promised, but feature-specific acceptance/evidence does not fully name it. **Ambiguous** means behavior is covered but an acceptance boundary or required scenario remains unclear. **Missing** means no responsible criterion or successor was found. No implementation or runtime behavior was tested.

There are 42 story rows: all 42 are explicit, with no delegated-only, ambiguous, or missing story obligations. The cross-cutting query integration and sequencing gap found in the review is resolved by explicit successor criteria and the user-approved 13 blocker change.

Since the initial pass, all 20 tickets gained completion boundaries. Classification, unclassified editing, exact ontology fields, and manual-support transition fixtures were clarified. The final user decision adds 11/12 to 13; 13 AC11 and 14 AC10 now specify later-kind query evidence. No known finding in this partition remains open; explicit documentary coverage is not runtime proof.

## Safety and preservation allocation

Issue 01 AC5–8 is the foundation for coherent snapshots, identity-checked operation locking, revision binding, confinement, staging, and guarded rollback. The exact baseline includes full acquisition-token/host/boot/PID/process-start ownership checks; unknown or abandoned locks and pending recovery block normal operations without automatic clearing; ownership-record failure precedes canonical writes; plans bind SHA-256 file bytes, destination absence, and relevant inventories; all proposed bytes/before-images/progress are staged on the same local filesystem before commit; and paths reject traversal, links/junctions, hard-linked writable files, aliases, and platform-equivalent collisions. Rollback restores only state still matching the transaction's expected post-change bytes, retaining evidence rather than overwriting intervening edits; completed cleanup failure is success with warning. This is not evidence that later operations correctly capture or restore their own graph, lock, and presentation files. Under the shared delivery requirements and issue README closure rules, every ticket must demonstrate safety for the operations it enables:

- Checks are read-only (09 AC6–7); failed/canceled acquisition and update preserve current snapshots and lock bytes (08 AC8–11; 10 AC5–6, AC9).
- Applications remain tied to reviewed bytes/revisions; uncertainty does not trigger blind retry or replanning (08 AC9; 10 AC5–6; 07 AC7–8; 13 AC8; 15 AC4–5).
- Locked snapshots remain read-only; editable copies use explicit import (03 AC5; 05 AC9; 08 AC10; 13 AC6).
- RDF edits preserve unknown statements and exact term identity, language, and datatype; forms do not infer unselected types, inverses, or optional data (05 AC2, AC6–8; 16 AC4–7; 17 AC4–6).
- Views/designs keep presentation separate from domain data; layout changes only the active presentation and preserves accepted coordinates on failure/cancel (13 AC4–6; 14 AC6; 15 AC2–5).
- Query, validation, and rendering use local content without implicit fetching; SPARQL policy is checked before engine invocation (04 AC7; 06 AC2, AC7; 08 AC4, AC10; 20 AC3).
- Removal/deletion has narrow ownership scope, leaves external references and unrelated values intact, exposes resulting validity, and does not silently repair, cascade, or collect independent resources (11 AC4–5; 12 AC2–7; 18 AC1–7; 19 AC2–7).
